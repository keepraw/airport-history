## Final disposition

Admitted five groups: ISL exchange, ISB relocation (month boundary), SXF/BER,
TSE/NQZ and KIV/RMO. The latter three are code changes at continuing physical
airfields; they do not create separate identities for terminals or names.

Eight candidates are deferred: Yantai (planned IATA coordinate change predates
the eventual flight move, actual implementation unconfirmed); Rostov (code and
flight-move dates require separate resolution); Lianyungang, Yibin, Luzhou and
Jining (no independently established code boundary collected); Dakar and Phnom
Penh (distinct-code replacements without verified allocation/withdrawal dates).
Their cited leads are not promoted to verified dataset sources. See the evidence
review for the date discrepancies and the specific missing evidence.
