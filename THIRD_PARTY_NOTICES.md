| Source material | Terms and formal use |
| --- | --- |
| OurAirports | Public-domain reference names, coordinates, and typed IATA and ICAO associations. [OurAirports data terms](https://ourairports.com/data/) · [Reference snapshot](https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data/tree/d97df1673cc5ba61a09b17ab5936091a669a04ca) |
| `airportsdata` and `mwgg/Airports` | MIT reference timezone data and Temindung name and coordinates. Full notices appear below. [airportsdata snapshot](https://github.com/mborsetti/airportsdata/tree/47ae282a8491758e6dafe28d4139539e561b54d5) · [mwgg/Airports snapshot](https://github.com/mwgg/Airports/tree/6dbaa83ab5efd193aa6d70f4f5bb1eb93ee821f8) |
| IANA tzdb 2025b | Public-domain geographical timezone identifiers from `zone.tab` and `zone1970.tab`. [IANA license](https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb-2025b/LICENSE) |
| Wikidata | CC0 structured statements for Kai Tak and Stapleton code history. [Wikidata terms](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing) · [Kai Tak](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q673202) · [Stapleton](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1436575) |
| FAA, NOAA, and GAO | U.S. federal works support certain U.S. airport history records. Embedded third-party material retains its original terms. [GovInfo policy](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies) · [NOAA policy](https://www.noaa.gov/information-technology/information-quality) · [GAO Denver report](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-AIMD-95-230/html/GAOREPORTS-AIMD-95-230.htm) |
| Airports Authority of India and ENAIRE | Publisher terms apply to AIP materials. The dataset uses factual observations about aerodrome designators and dates. [AAI aeronautical information](https://aim-india.aai.aero/) · [ENAIRE aeronautical information](https://aip.enaire.es/) |
| PRC administrative documents | Article 5(1) scope applies to the individual official decisions and notices. Maps, EIA annexes, charts, images, and other website content retain their terms. [Lianyungang notice](https://www.lyg.gov.cn/zglygzfmhwz/szfbgswj1/content/89a86e48-47a7-4067-9f88-65b0cc1d04e8.html) · [Lianyungang approval](https://www.mee.gov.cn/xxgk2018/xxgk/xxgk11/201901/t20190121_690274.html) · [Luzhou approval](https://www.mee.gov.cn/gkml/sthjbgw/spwj1/201404/t20140403_270104.htm) · [Yibin approval](https://www.mee.gov.cn/gkml/sthjbgw/spwj1/201404/t20140403_270103.htm) |

Factual annotations also draw on government, airline, airport and press
publications, including Astana and Pranoto official-name materials, IATA and
ASECNA Dakar notices, and Pakistan and Türkiye AIP reproductions. Publisher
rights remain with each issuer.
